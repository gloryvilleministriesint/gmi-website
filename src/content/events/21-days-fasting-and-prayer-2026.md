For 21 days, from Monday 30 November to Sunday 20 December, the GMI family sets time apart to seek God together in fasting and prayer.

> “Turn ye even to me with all your heart, and with fasting.” (Joel 2:12, KJV)

**Where and when:** we meet at the church every day, **Monday to Friday**. The meeting time will be announced soon.

**Prayer Convocation week:** from Friday 4 to Sunday 6 December we pray together at the [Prayer Convocation](/calendar/prayer-convocation-2026) at the Foursquare Gospel Camp Ground, Ajebo. Please register for it ahead of time.

The fast ends on **Sunday 20 December**, which is [All White Sunday](/calendar/all-white-sunday-2026).
